our story ✦

Handmade, with a whole lot of heart

The Crafty Cornerr began at a kitchen table — a few skeins of wool, a pile of pearls, and a wish to make Indian homes a little warmer. What started as festive gifts for family quietly grew into a tiny studio crafting rangoli, toran and florals for homes all across the country.

Every piece is still made by hand, one at a time. No machines, no mass production — just slow, careful craft and colours chosen to make your corner feel unmistakably yours.
