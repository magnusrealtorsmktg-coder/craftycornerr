Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated 25 August 2026

Every piece is made by hand, often to order, so our policy is built around exchanges rather than returns. If something arrives damaged or incorrect, we will put it right.

Cancellations

Orders cannot be cancelled once placed. We begin preparing and packing your order straight away, and for custom pieces work starts almost immediately. If you have made a genuine mistake — the wrong item, a wrong address — message us on WhatsApp immediately with your order number. We cannot promise anything, but if the parcel has not yet been dispatched we will do what we can.

Please film your unboxing

A clear, continuous unboxing video is required for any exchange claim. Start recording before the parcel is opened and keep filming until the item is fully unwrapped, without cuts or pauses. This is the only way we can tell whether a piece was damaged in transit, and it is what lets us claim against the courier on your behalf.

Without that video we are unable to process an exchange, so please film every parcel before opening it — it takes a few seconds and protects you completely.

Damaged, defective or incorrect items — exchange within 48 hours

If your piece arrives broken, faulty, or is not what you ordered, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with:

  • Your order number
  • The unboxing video
  • Photographs of the item and the outer packaging

Once we have reviewed these we will send a replacement at no cost to you, including return postage where the original needs to come back. We aim to respond within 2–3 working days. Claims raised after 48 hours cannot be accepted.

Refunds

We do not offer refunds. Our remedy for a damaged or incorrect piece is an exchange, as set out above.

There is one exception: if your parcel is confirmed lost in transit by the courier and cannot be replaced, we will refund the price of the goods. Shipping charges are not refunded. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay and usually reach your account within 5–7 working days, depending on your bank. We will confirm by email as soon as one is issued.

Custom and made-to-order pieces

Custom pieces are made specifically for you and follow the same terms: they cannot be cancelled or refunded, and the only exception is a parcel confirmed lost in transit. If a custom piece arrives damaged or differs from what was agreed with you, we will remake or exchange it — the unboxing video and the 48-hour window apply here too.

What we cannot exchange

  • Change of mind. Because every piece is handmade, we do not accept returns or exchanges simply because you have changed your mind.
  • Items damaged after delivery through normal wear, misuse or improper storage.
  • Claims made without an unboxing video.
  • Claims raised more than 48 hours after delivery.

Slight variations are not defects

Handmade pieces vary a little in colour, finish and size, and screens show colours differently. These natural variations are part of the character of handmade work and are not grounds for an exchange.

How to raise a claim

Message +91 70309 24448 on WhatsApp, or email thecraftycornerr26@gmail.com, with your order number, the unboxing video and photographs. We reply within 2–3 working days and will keep you updated until it is resolved.

Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated 25 August 2026

Where we ship

We ship across India. We do not currently ship internationally — if you are outside India and would like something made, message us on WhatsApp and we will see what is possible.

What it costs

  • Orders of ₹999 and above — free shipping
  • Orders below ₹999 — a flat ₹79

The exact amount is always shown in your bag before you pay. There are no hidden charges and nothing is collected on delivery.

How long it takes

  • Ready-to-ship pieces are dispatched within 2–4 working days.
  • Custom and made-to-order pieces take longer — we agree a timeline with you when you order.
  • Once dispatched, delivery time depends on the courier and your location — usually up to 15 days.

Festive periods such as Diwali are busier than usual and dispatch can take a little longer. We will always tell you if your order is affected.

Tracking your order

You will receive an order confirmation by email as soon as your payment succeeds, and tracking details by email once your parcel is dispatched. You can also message us on WhatsApp with your order number at any time and we will check for you.

Delivery attempts and wrong addresses

Please double-check your address and phone number at checkout — couriers usually call before delivering. If a parcel is returned to us because the address was incorrect or nobody was available, we can resend it once the re-delivery charge is paid.

If something goes missing

If tracking has not moved for several days, or a parcel is marked delivered but has not arrived, contact us on WhatsApp or at thecraftycornerr26@gmail.com as soon as you notice. We will raise it with the courier and keep you updated until it is resolved.
